Study method, constants & formula reference

Use the same method for every problem

1. Sketch and choose a system. 2. Name known quantities and convert units. 3. State the model and its assumptions. 4. Solve symbolically before substituting. 5. Check signs, units, scale, and limiting cases. Try each example before opening its solution, then explain your self-check answer aloud.
